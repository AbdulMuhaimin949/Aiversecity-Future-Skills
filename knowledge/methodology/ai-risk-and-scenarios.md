# AI Risk, Job Safety and the three disruption scenarios

## AI Risk and Job Safety

AI Risk (AI exposure) estimates how much AI may affect a role's tasks. It is not a prediction of job loss. Job Safety (AI augmentation potential) estimates how much AI may help people do the work. It is not a guarantee of employment. Both are shown as percentages. For roles outside the technology sector, the exposure levels follow the relative ordering of sectors in the International Labour Organization's Working Paper 96, "Generative AI and Jobs" (2023); the exact numbers are assumptions.

## Skill resilience badges

Each skill gets a score from the roles that need it: average Job Safety minus a scenario weight × average(AI Risk × (1 − Job Safety)). The penalty is zero in Low Impact and grows in Moderate Impact and High Impact. The top half of skills is marked AI-Resilient; the rest are marked Exposed. The badges are relative and describe roles, not specific tasks.

## The three scenarios

- Low Impact (baseline): Today's hiring baseline. The current trend continues. Demand comes from a Ridge autoregressive forecast trained on quarterly demand indices, four quarters ahead. No extra AI shock is applied.
- Moderate Impact (moderate): Quick adoption of automation. The pipeline's moderate scenario (+6% × net AI augmentation synergy), then a displacement adjustment of 0.15 × AI Risk × (1 − Job Safety). The 90% uncertainty range is widened by 25%.
- High Impact (fast): Rapid transformation across all sectors. The pipeline's fast scenario (+14% × net AI augmentation synergy, including the green-digital transition), then a stronger displacement adjustment of 0.40 × AI Risk × (1 − Job Safety). The 90% uncertainty range is widened by 60%.

Roles are labelled Rising, Steady or Slowing by splitting projected change into thirds within each scenario.

## How to read projections

All projections are estimates from simulated quarterly demand indices anchored to the Malaysia Critical Occupations List (MyCOL), on a normalised vacancy index with base 100. They are conditional scenarios, not predictions of any individual's future, and should be read together with the 90% uncertainty range.
