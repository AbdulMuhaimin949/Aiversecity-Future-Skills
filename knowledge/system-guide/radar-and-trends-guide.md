# Future Skills Radar & Scenarios / Radar Kemahiran Masa Hadapan & Senario / 技能雷达与AI影响情景指南

## Feature Details / Butiran Ciri / 功能详解
### English
- **What it does:** Tracks labor demand trends for 240 occupations and 255 skills across 20 Malaysian industries under three distinct AI adoption scenarios.
- **Where to find it:** The floating Radar bubble at the bottom of the screen (drag or tap anytime) or full view at `/radar`.
- **How to read it:**
  - **Rising roles:** Demand is growing relative to other occupations in that scenario.
  - **Slowing roles:** Demand is decelerating or routine tasks are being automated.
  - **90% Range:** Statistical uncertainty band—future demand is expected to stay within this shaded band 90% of the time.
  - **3 Scenarios:**
    - Low Impact (Baseline): Today's normal hiring trend without sudden extra AI shocks.
    - Moderate Impact: Fast adoption of automation across sectors.
    - High Impact: Rapid transformation where AI exposure and augmentation have the strongest impact.
- **Common confusion:** "Does slowing mean the job is dead?" No. Slowing indicates routine tasks are shifting or hiring growth is decelerating compared to other roles.
- **Informal questions:** "what is this radar for ah", "what is rising slowing", "apa fungsi radar", "ni radar buat apa", "这个Radar是干嘛的？", "这个Radar是做么的？", "我看不懂这个Radar".

### Bahasa Melayu
- **Fungsi:** Memantau arah aliran permintaan pasaran bagi 240 pekerjaan dan kemahiran merentas 20 sektor di Malaysia.
- **Lokasi:** Butang gelembung terapung Radar di skrin (boleh ditarik ke mana-mana) atau halaman penuh di `/radar`.
- **Cara membaca:**
  - **Peranan Meningkat (Rising):** Permintaan bertumbuh berbanding pekerjaan lain.
  - **Peranan Perlahan (Slowing):** Permintaan berkurangan atau tugas lazim diautomasikan.
  - **Julat 90%:** Jalur ketidaktentuan statistik (90% unjuran dijangka berada di dalam julat ini).
  - **3 Senario:** Low Impact (Garis asas), Moderate Impact (Automasi sederhana), High Impact (Transformasi pantas).

### 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese & Malaysian Colloquial)
- **核心功能：** 监测马来西亚20大核心行业中240个职业与255项技能在3种不同AI情景下的市场走势。
- **页面入口：** 页面右下方的可拖动浮动雷达气泡（Assistive Touch 风格），或点击直达 `/radar`。
- **图表与术语解读：**
  - **上升职位 (Rising)：** 相对于同情景下的其他岗位，招聘需求与重要性呈上升趋势。
  - **放缓职位 (Slowing)：** 需求放缓或大量常规日常任务正被自动化系统替代。
  - **90%预测区间 (90% Range)：** 统计不确定性区间阴影带，表明预测有90%概率落入该波动范围内。
  - **3大AI采用情景：**
    - **低影响 (Low Impact / Baseline)：** 维持现状的正常基准走势，无突发外部技术冲击。
    - **中度影响 (Moderate Impact)：** 各行业快速引入自动化和数字化工具。
    - **高影响 (High Impact)：** 深度转型，AI暴露度与增强作用发挥最强效果。
- **常见误区：** “放缓是否代表这个工作会消失？” 不代表失业，而是工作任务正在重构，提示您需要及时考取补充抗AI冲击的新兴技能。
- **常见提问：** "这个Radar是干嘛的？", "这个Radar是做么的？", "我看不懂这个Radar", "上升和放缓是什么意思？"。
