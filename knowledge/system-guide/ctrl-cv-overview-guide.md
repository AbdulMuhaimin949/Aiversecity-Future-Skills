# CTRL CV System Guide / Panduan Sistem CTRL CV / CTRL CV 系统完整使用指南

## 1. Platform Overview / Gambaran Keseluruhan Platform / 平台核心概述
### English
- **What it does:** CTRL CV is a free career navigation platform designed for Malaysian students, job seekers, and workers. It evaluates your skills, projects career readiness under 3 AI disruption scenarios, and generates a clear reskilling pathway using verified HRD Corp e-LATiH courses.
- **Where to find it:** Main navigation header and step-by-step workflow: Step 1 Profile (`/profile`) -> Step 2 Matches (`/matches`) -> Step 3 Pathway (`/pathway/:id`), with a floating interactive Radar bubble (`/radar`).
- **How to use it:** Build your profile by uploading your CV or picking skills, review your personalized matches, and build your reskilling pathway.
- **Common confusion:** "Does the system guarantee a job?" No. The platform provides indicative estimations based on MyCOL and economic models to help you make informed reskilling decisions.
- **Troubleshooting:** All profile data is saved locally in your browser (`localStorage`). No private resume files are stored on our servers.

### Bahasa Melayu
- **Fungsi:** CTRL CV ialah platform navigasi kerjaya percuma untuk bakat Malaysia bagi menghadapi era automasi dan AI.
- **Lokasi:** Alur 3 langkah utama: Langkah 1 Profil (`/profile`) -> Langkah 2 Padanan (`/matches`) -> Langkah 3 Laluan (`/pathway/:id`), bersama butang terapung Radar (`/radar`).
- **Cara guna:** Muat naik CV (PDF) atau pilih kemahiran, semak peranan yang sesuai di Padanan, dan jana laluan pembelajaran.
- **Kekeliruan:** Bukan jaminan pekerjaan; ia unjuran berpandukan data MyCOL dan analisis matematik.
- **Penyelesaian masalah:** Data disimpan dalam pelayar anda (`localStorage`). Fail CV tidak disimpan di pelayan.
