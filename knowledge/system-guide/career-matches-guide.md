# Step 2 Career Matches & Scoring / Langkah 2 Padanan Kerjaya / 第2步 职位匹配与打分机制

## Feature Details / Butiran Ciri / 功能详解
### English
- **What it does:** Ranks 240 future-ready occupations against your profile using a transparent mathematical formula, highlighting matched skills, skill gaps, AI Risk, and Job Safety.
- **Where to find it:** Step 2 in the navigation or `/matches`.
- **How scoring works:**
  - Skill coverage (45%): Required and preferred skills you already hold.
  - Proficiency alignment (25%): Your self-reported skill rating vs required level.
  - Education & experience readiness (15%): Academic fit and working tenure.
  - Interests & labor demand trend (15%): Target sectors and market forecast.
- **Key Terms:**
  - **AI Risk (AI exposure):** The share of a role's tasks that can be assisted or impacted by AI. Not a prediction of redundancy.
  - **Job Safety (augmentation):** How much AI acts as a helpful multiplier rather than replacement.
- **Troubleshooting:**
  - "Why is my match list empty?" You must build and click "Save Profile" in Step 1 first.
  - "How can I increase my score?" Add more skills or adjust your proficiency levels in Step 1.
- **Informal questions:** "why no matches ah", "kenapa xde match", "how come no match", "macam mana skor dikira", "为什么没有match？", "匹配度怎么算的？", "我要怎样看工作配对？".

### Bahasa Melayu
- **Fungsi:** Menyusun 240 pekerjaan masa depan mengikut kesesuaian kemahiran profil anda melalui formula telus.
- **Lokasi:** Tab Langkah 2 di menu atas atau `/matches`.
- **Formula:** Liputan kemahiran (45%), Ketepatan tahap kemahiran (25%), Kelayakan & Pengalaman (15%), Minat & Trend permintaan (15%).
- **Istilah Utama:**
  - **Risiko AI (AI Risk):** Tahap pendedahan tugas kepada AI (bukan jaminan hilang kerja).
  - **Keselamatan Kerja (Job Safety):** Tahap peranan AI membantu memperkasakan manusia.
- **Penyelesaian masalah:** Jika tiada padanan keluar, pastikan anda telah menekan "Simpan Profil" di Langkah 1.

### 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese & Malaysian Colloquial)
- **核心功能：** 基于您的个人技能档案，从240个未来职业中智能匹配最适合您的岗位，清晰列出已掌握技能、技能缺口、AI风险指数与工作安全度。
- **页面入口：** 顶部导航【第2步 职位匹配】或访问 `/matches`。
- **透明评分公式构成：**
  - 核心/首选技能覆盖率（45%）
  - 技能熟练度匹配度（25%）
  - 学历背景与工作年限适配度（15%）
  - 个人意向行业与市场招聘趋势（15%）
- **核心指标解读：**
  - **AI 风险 (AI Risk)：** 衡量该岗位工作任务受AI重塑或影响的程度，**绝非**失业概率预测。
  - **工作安全度 (Job Safety)：** 反映AI作为工具赋能辅助人类、提升生产力而非直接取代人类的程度。
- **疑难解答：**
  - “为什么没有匹配结果 (match)？” 请确保在第1步【个人档案】中点击了“保存个人档案 (Save Profile)”。
  - “如何提高匹配分数？” 可以在个人档案中补充您的其他技能或调高熟练度评级。
- **常见提问：** "为什么没有match？", "我要怎样看工作配对？", "分数是怎么算出来的？", "AI风险高是不是代表会失业？"。
