# Step 1 Profile & CV Upload / Langkah 1 Profil & Muat Naik CV / 第1步 个人档案与简历上传

## Feature Details / Butiran Ciri / 功能详解
### English
- **What it does:** Allows you to define your verified skills, proficiency levels (1–5), education qualification, years of experience, weekly study availability, and sector interests.
- **Where to find it:** Step 1 tab in the top navigation or navigate directly to `/profile`.
- **How to use it:**
  1. Method A: Upload a PDF resume (under 5MB) or paste text, click "Scan CV", review extracted skills, adjust levels, and click "Save Profile".
  2. Method B: Load a demo synthetic persona (Student or Professional) for instant testing.
  3. Method C: Search and select manually from 255 standardized skills.
- **Common confusion:** "Why didn't the scan pick up every word?" The scanner matches against the canonical 255 MyCOL-anchored skill library. If a specific keyword was missed, simply click "Pick skills" to add it manually.
- **Troubleshooting:** PDF must be text-based (not scanned image or handwritten), under 5MB. Clear browser cache or reload if upload stalls.
- **Informal questions:** "cannot upload cv lah", "why cannot upload my CV", "xleh upload cv", "fail sangkut", "我upload不到CV", "简历上传不了", "怎么加技能".

### Bahasa Melayu
- **Fungsi:** Membina profil kemahiran anda daripada 255 kemahiran standard, tahap kemahiran (1–5), pendidikan, pengalaman dan masa belajar mingguan.
- **Lokasi:** Tab Langkah 1 di menu atas atau terus ke `/profile`.
- **Cara guna:**
  1. Kaedah A: Muat naik PDF resume (bawah 5MB) atau tampal teks resume, tekan "Imbas CV", semak kemahiran, dan tekan "Simpan Profil".
  2. Kaedah B: Guna Persona Demo (Pelajar atau Profesional).
  3. Kaedah C: Cari dan pilih kemahiran secara manual di bahagian "Pilih kemahiran".
- **Penyelesaian masalah:** Pastikan fail PDF mengandungi teks yang boleh disalin dan bersaiz kurang 5MB. Jika ada kemahiran terlepas, tambah secara manual.
- **Soalan lazim santai:** "camne nak upload cv", "xleh upload", "resume sangkut", "cmne nak tambah skill".

### 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese & Malaysian Colloquial)
- **核心功能：** 录入并管理您的技能库、技能熟练度（1–5级）、最高学历、工作年限、每周可用学习时长及意向行业。
- **页面入口：** 顶部导航第一项【第1步 个人档案】或访问 `/profile`。
- **使用方法：**
  1. 方式一：点击上传简历区域上传 PDF（5MB以内）或粘贴文本，点击“扫描简历”，核对系统提取的技能与熟练度后，点击“保存个人档案”。
  2. 方式二：点击“演示角色 (Persona)”快速加载学生或在职样本。
  3. 方式三：点击“手动挑选技能”，直接从255项标准化技能库中搜索勾选。
- **常见误区：** “为什么简历里的某句话没被提取出来？” 扫描器专门将文本映射到官方收录的255项标准技能中。如有遗漏，点击“手动挑选技能”一秒即可添加。
- **疑难解答：** 请确认 PDF 为电子版文本（非拍照扫描件或纯图片），且文件小于 5MB。保存后数据完全存放在您自己的浏览器本地，不会上传服务器。
- **常见提问：** "我upload不到CV", "简历上传失败怎么办", "怎么加技能", "可以在哪里编辑资料？"。
