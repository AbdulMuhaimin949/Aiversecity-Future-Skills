## Overview

### Problem

Students often struggle to understand which career paths suit their interests and abilities, especially as artificial intelligence and automation continue to change the job market. Career information is scattered across different sources, making it difficult for students to identify emerging opportunities and the skills employers may require. Students need an accessible way to explore career options and make more informed decisions about their future.

### Solution

Aiversecity is an AI-powered career exploration platform designed to guide students from self-discovery to career planning. It combines user profiles, career trend exploration, career recommendations, and personalised learning pathways in one interface. The platform aims to make future career information easier to understand while providing supporting sources and context for its recommendations.

### Key Features

- **Career Profile:** Helps users explore their interests, abilities, and current skills.
- **Career Radar:** Presents emerging career trends and information about future skill demands.
- **Career Matches:** Recommends potential career paths based on the user's profile.
- **Learning Pathway:** Helps users identify skills and learning steps relevant to their chosen career direction.
- **Evidence and Sources:** Provides supporting references for career-related information and forecasts where available.
- **SDG Alignment:** Supports SDG 4 – Quality Education by helping students access career exploration and skills-development information.

## Challenges & Learnings

### Challenges

- Making career and labour-market information understandable to students with different levels of technical knowledge.
- Designing a user experience that presents useful information without overwhelming new users.
- Integrating application components and AI services while maintaining reliable data flow.

### Learnings

- Career recommendations should be understandable and supported by relevant evidence.
- Clear user journeys are important when presenting complex AI-related information.
- Testing the application end to end is essential to verify that its features work as intended.

## Future Improvements

- Improve onboarding so first-time users can complete their profiles and understand their results more easily.
- Expand career data sources and improve the transparency of recommendations and forecasts.
- Personalise learning pathways based on users' interests, existing skills, and career goals.
